# Nimbus Product Overview

Nimbus is a B2B productivity app for small teams to manage projects, tasks, and client communication.

## Plans
- **Trial**: 14-day free trial, no credit card required
- **Starter**: EUR 12/user/month
- **Pro**: EUR 24/user/month
- **Business**: EUR 48/user/month

## Core workflow
1. A user signs up and creates or joins an organization.
2. During onboarding, they are prompted to create a project, invite teammates, and connect a calendar.
3. Teams use projects, tasks, comments, and client updates.
4. Trials convert to paid plans when an organization upgrades.

## Known business problem
Trial signups are increasing, but the percentage of trials converting to paid plans has remained flat for the last 90 days.

## Customer context
Customer Success reports that new customers often understand the value of Nimbus but get stuck during initial setup, especially when inviting teammates and creating their first project.
